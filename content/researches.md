## Current Focus

### New Strategies for Human Embryo Evaluation

Clinical embryo assessment still relies primarily on morphology, most commonly the Gardner grading system and related time-lapse imaging approaches. Preimplantation genetic testing (PGT), by contrast, examines DNA-level and genomic variation. New strategies—including liquid biopsy, metabolomics, secretomics, and fluorescence lifetime imaging microscopy (FLIM)—offer considerable promise, although their technical development and clinical validation are still in progress.

## Projects at UCLA

### Placental Organoids · Sex-Chromosome Evolution in Planarians

1. Used CRISPR–Cas9 to direct the differentiation of induced pluripotent stem (iPS) cells into primitive endoderm (PE) and epiblast (EPI) lineages, followed by the generation of organoids that recapitulate features of placental tissue morphology.

2. Studied planarians maintained through multiple generations of artificial inbreeding and backcrossing. Chromosome 1 contains extensive conserved regions largely unaffected by mating, which may be closely associated with the emergence and evolution of animal sex chromosomes.

## Projects at People’s Hospital, Zhengzhou University

### ART · Embryonic Epigenetics · Reproductive Medicine

1. Used mouse embryos to examine how IVF culture conditions affect lipid metabolism and epigenetic modifications, with particular attention to the methylation of imprinted genes.

2. Conducted clinical reproductive research, including retrospective case–control studies of infertility subgroups, meta-analyses of ART procedures, and studies of medical ethics and reproductive nursing in China.

3. In collaboration with a livestock cloning company in Zhengzhou, studied bovine embryos produced by somatic cell nuclear transfer (SCNT) and tissue samples from SCNT-derived calves to investigate changes in the methylation of imprinted genes.

## PhD Project

### Imprinted Genes and Metabolism in Embryos and Placentas

Using mouse embryos and placentas, I analyzed the proteome of IVF-derived zygotes, methylation patterns of imprinted genes, and the placental lipidome. The work explored connections between epigenetic regulation and metabolism and the mechanisms underlying these relationships.

## M.S. Project

### Genetic Susceptibility to Complex Diseases

I collected blood samples from hundreds of patients with rheumatoid arthritis or ankylosing spondylitis, along with matched controls; extracted DNA; and genotyped single-nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) in susceptibility genes. I then used genetic association analyses to identify disease-specific genotypes and haplotypes and investigated potential mechanisms.

## B.S. Project

### Drug Resistance Mechanisms in Candida albicans

Cultured drug-resistant and wild-type strains of Candida albicans, qualifying the phenotypes, extracted RNA and compared the expression levels and patterns of drug efflux genes and genes in related pathways.
